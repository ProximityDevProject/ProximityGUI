Changelog

What changed and when. The library tells players about a new version by itself.

1.3.0 - a key for anything

  • Right-click any element: type an exact value, open its options, or give it a key even where the script gave none
  • 86 own Proximity icons, gallery cards with rarity and search, Segmented, Stepper and Canvas elements
  • Sliders with fine Shift drag, danger zones, marks and throttled callbacks; hold-to-confirm buttons; badge kinds and auto-expiring NEW
  • Unload takes back everything: connections, loops, properties, hooks, instances and binds made through the library
  • Batch, Use (plugins), Element, GetElement, Deprecated, ExportFlags, Localize, per-game config profiles, config compare, JSON and Lua export
  • Paper, Midnight, Graphite Blue and Mono Glass themes; high contrast, corner style, accent from your avatar, text size, theme import from Rayfield, Linoria and Orion
  • 3D planet logo; Nebula, Aurora and Comets backdrops; resize from every corner; menu blur, FPS cap, cursors and window snapping
  • Sounds: clicks, notifications, 16 hit sounds and 12 hit markers, cached once; toggle hit sounds
  • Tool widgets: session data, event feed, chat log, friends here, looking at me, inventory viewer, music player; crosshair editor, ESP preview, Discord card, code editor
  • Phones: bottom tab bar, touch density, safe areas, swipe-away notifications; gamepad vibration
  • Error report and self test in Settings; Rayfield, Orion and Linoria option names are understood
  • Website: Russian, light theme, version picker, search highlights, every executor with status history, community page, builder projects and templates, offline docs, link previews
  • README and API reference in English and Russian, migration guides from Rayfield, Orion and Linoria, a hub template

1.2.9 - smoother navigation

  • A highlight pill glides between tabs; pages arrive from the direction you travel and the title slides in
  • No more one-column flicker while the window animates
  • Starfield and a glowing progress bar on the new loading screens

1.2.8 - springs and a new look

  • Menu animations rewritten on a physics spring: a natural settle, and pressing the key mid-way turns them around smoothly
  • Brightness ramp, glowing reveal edges, a shine and rim pulse when the window lands, page content rising in; new Glitch style
  • Loader planet with cloud belts and a ring of six ringlets
  • New home page with live previews of the loading screens and the menu animations; a new planet with storms and ringlets

1.2.7 - new loading screens

  • Orbit: orbits with moons, 96 ticks light up with real progress, the menu grows from the centre
  • Eclipse: a moon covers a white sun as it loads; at 100% the corona flares and a diamond ring flashes
  • Constellation: stars appear and lines join them one by one as it loads
  • Terminal: a boot console types out every step with an ASCII bar and time left
  • Settings > Loading screen, or Loading = { Style = "Eclipse" }

1.2.6 - menu animations

  • 9 new ways for the menu to appear and hide: Rise, Zoom, Drop, Slide, Swing, Wipe, Unfold, Scan and Fade
  • Settings > Menu animation, Animation = "Swing" or Window:SetAnimation("Wipe")
  • Pressing the key mid-animation turns it around smoothly

1.2.5

  • The floating planet button shows only on phones; on PC the menu key opens the window

1.2.4 - tested in game

  • The intro can no longer skip its own assembly when the script is slow to start
  • Once whole, the planet's tiles melt into a smooth sphere
  • Finer, softer ring without bright seams

1.2.3 - softer ring, prettier assembly

  • The ring fades evenly from white on the lit side to grey on the far side, and the far side stays visible
  • Points come in white, grey and near-black and spiral in around the planet's axis in the order the surface forms

1.2.2 - built from points

  • The loader planet is really assembled: about 1500 points land on it while it turns, and the surface and the ring are laid down piece by piece exactly where they land
  • Planet and ring turn together around the planet's own tilted axis, with bands and storms so you can see it spin
  • The burst waits for the planet to finish forming
  • Website: thousands of points land on the spinning 3D planet and fuse into its surface; no more cut-off planet on phones

1.2.1 - a real planet

  • The planet loader is a drawn 3D model now: points gather into a dark sphere with a lit crescent edge and a tilted glowing ring, then it spins while the menu builds
  • Pick the loading screen in Settings: Planet, Menu skeleton or None. In code: Loading = { Style = "Skeleton" }
  • Menu skeleton: diagonal shimmer, a small orbit spinner, time left, and a soft wipe that reveals the finished menu
  • The topbar planet button moves past buttons that games and other scripts put into the topbar
  • Website: the hero planet is a live 3D render that the points condense into; the Builder is a real editor with New element, an inspector, reorder, duplicate, undo and download

1.2.0 - the planet intro

  • Loading screen: the screen dims, a 3D planet assembles from points, spins with real progress and time left, then bursts and the window grows out of it
  • Key system with keys, your own check or a URL, a "Get a key" button, attempt limit and remembered keys. KeySystem = true / false
  • The window opens from the planet button next to the Roblox menu and folds back into it
  • Icons only navigation, mini mode, custom background image or gradient, density from compact to touch
  • 9 toggle styles, about 50 fonts including Monocraft, chips in multi dropdowns, soft glow on the active tab and switches, red stripe on risky sections
  • Keyboard and gamepad navigation, remembered scroll position of every page
  • Keybind combos like Ctrl+Shift+X and binds on Shift or Ctrl alone; the chip shows the bind mode
  • Notification center with unread counter, what's new dialog, update check
  • Player list element, range and select filters in tables
  • Widgets: player panel with a 3D model, FPS and ping graph, stopwatch, your own HUD panels; watermark gets 16 items and a live FPS line
  • Configs: scrambled files with a checksum, backup before overwrite, undo delete, short share codes
  • OnError, readable option warnings, debug overlay, automatic FPS saving mode, notice when an older copy is replaced
  • This website with a code generator and a live executor table

1.1.0 - the planet

  • The logo is the Proximity planet, plus a faint planet in the corner of the window
  • Icon on every row and 130 readable icon names
  • Settings got icons, natural wording in English and Russian, plain hyphens everywhere

1.0.1 - first in-game test

  • Popups and dragging no longer shift by the Roblox topbar height
  • Option panels, range slider label, notification sizing and loading progress fixed

1.0.0 - first release

  • Window, tabs, sections, 30 elements, configs, themes, Ctrl+K, undo and redo, widgets
