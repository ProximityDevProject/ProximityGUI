Changelog

What changed and when. The library tells players about a new version by itself.

1.2.0 - the planet intro

  • Loading screen: the screen dims, a 3D planet assembles from points, spins with real progress and time left, then bursts and the window grows out of it
  • Key system with keys, your own check or a URL, a "Get a key" button, attempt limit and remembered keys. KeySystem = true / false
  • The window opens from the planet button next to the Roblox menu and folds back into it
  • Icons only navigation, mini mode, custom background image or gradient, density from compact to touch
  • 9 toggle styles, about 50 fonts including Monocraft, chips in multi dropdowns, soft glow on the active tab and switches, red stripe on risky sections
  • Keyboard and gamepad navigation, remembered scroll position of every page
  • Keybind combos like Ctrl+Shift+X and binds on Shift or Ctrl alone; the chip shows the bind mode
  • Notification center with unread counter, what's new dialog, update check
  • Player list element, range and select filters in tables
  • Widgets: player panel with a 3D model, FPS and ping graph, stopwatch, your own HUD panels; watermark gets 16 items and a live FPS line
  • Configs: scrambled files with a checksum, backup before overwrite, undo delete, short share codes
  • OnError, readable option warnings, debug overlay, automatic FPS saving mode, notice when an older copy is replaced
  • This website with a code generator and a live executor table

1.1.0 - the planet

  • The logo is the Proximity planet, plus a faint planet in the corner of the window
  • Icon on every row and 130 readable icon names
  • Settings got icons, natural wording in English and Russian, plain hyphens everywhere

1.0.1 - first in-game test

  • Popups and dragging no longer shift by the Roblox topbar height
  • Option panels, range slider label, notification sizing and loading progress fixed

1.0.0 - first release

  • Window, tabs, sections, 30 elements, configs, themes, Ctrl+K, undo and redo, widgets
